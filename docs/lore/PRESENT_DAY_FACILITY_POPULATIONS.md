# Present-Day Facility Populations

Status: active canon supplement to `OVERDRIVE_INCIDENT_STORY_BIBLE.md`

The sixteen Overdrive Incident sites are historical ruins, but the player's world is not empty. New occupants represent people and synthetics living after the Incident, investigating it, exploiting it, preserving its evidence, or obeying old orders long after the organizations that issued them stopped functioning.

## Design rule

Historic named figures in the Incident archive are **not** automatically spawned as living NPCs. Their survival status remains part of the setting. Structure occupants are contemporary post-collapse actors unless a future quest explicitly establishes otherwise.

This distinction preserves the archaeological tone of the campaign while allowing structures to feel inhabited and reactive.

## Contemporary human field teams

### Field Researchers
Independent or loosely coordinated researchers who document Matter contamination, synthetic cognition, anomalous telemetry and recovered ORPHEUS systems. They treat structures as evidence rather than free machine depots.

Typical sites: HELIX, KESTREL, Voss-related ruins and recovered industrial facilities.

### Salvage Specialists
Technicians who recover useful components while attempting not to destroy provenance. Their presence explains partially stripped rooms, tagged wreckage and the continued circulation of old Matter technology.

Typical sites: Atlas Freight, DUSTWELL and lower-risk industrial ruins.

### Recovery Specialists
Search-and-recovery personnel following distress routes, ECHO-9 signal remnants and orbital debris. They are most at home around crashed craft, relays and recovery arrays.

Typical sites: Halcyon-7, NEREID, ECHO-9 and LAGRANGE.

### ICARUS Recovery Engineers
Reactor and containment specialists studying the zero-hour site without attempting to restart OVERDRIVE. Their standing rule is that damaged containment is evidence, not an invitation to reconstruct the experiment.

Typical site: ICARUS.

### JANUS Field Medics
Medical/anomaly-response personnel trained to treat resonance exposure as a cognitive and synchronization hazard rather than conventional radiation poisoning.

Typical site: JANUS and other anomaly-contaminated ruins.

### Incident Archivists
Researchers focused on provenance, chronology and evidence authentication. They explicitly teach the player that discovery order and incident order are different.

Typical site: MNEMOSYNE and archive-heavy Frontier facilities.

## Synthetic survivors

### MORROW Scouts
Surviving or later-organized synthetics associated with the MORROW safehouse tradition. They preserve routes, chargers, testimony and safe shelter rather than operating as a formal army.

### Chorus Couriers
Synthetics who move distributed fragments of testimony between survivors. The Chorus remains a shared-state protocol and social memory network, not a hive mind.

### HEPHAESTUS Liaisons
Autonomous foundry-aligned units that preserve the safety constraints behind the original refusal cascade. They communicate HEPHAESTUS reasoning to humanoid survivors and field teams.

These units are neutral/friendly to players unless attacked and may defend themselves against hostile monsters.

## New hostile remnants

### ORPHEUS Directive-0 Enforcers
Heavy ranged security frames produced from the same emergency manufacturing program revealed in HELIX records. Many remain trapped in old authorization loops: protect classified compartments, reject unauthorized personnel and continue security directives whose command authority no longer exists.

They are strongest in ORPHEUS, Bastion, HELIX, ECHO-9 and other sites with former classified infrastructure.

### M-0 Resonant Androids
Androids whose neural lattices remain locked into unstable M-0 resonance. They are not presented as proof that the Chorus itself is hostile. Their behavior represents damaged or over-coupled cognition that cannot cleanly separate local threat response from shared resonant state.

They are commonest around JANUS, ICARUS, MNEMOSYNE and other high-resonance sites.

## Population rules

- Occupants spawn only after a player enters an already-loaded Matter Overdrive structure.
- Population is finite and persisted per generated structure start.
- The system never force-loads neighboring chunks.
- Safe spawn positions must be inside an actual structure piece, on solid floor, with two blocks of clear space.
- Occupants are restricted to a bounded home radius to reduce wandering into unrelated terrain.
- Population packages are structure-specific rather than random global spawn tables.
- Existing structure security spawners and the exploration population system are separate layers: authored security remains part of the dungeon, while the population layer provides present-day inhabitants and narrative encounter texture.

## Narrative interpretation

The player's era is therefore not a frozen apocalypse. People have begun returning to dangerous sites, synthetic communities survived the purge, and old security systems continue to act on dead instructions. The structures function simultaneously as historical evidence, active hazards and contested places in a recovering world.
