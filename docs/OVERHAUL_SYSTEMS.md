# Player-driven technology overhaul

## Quantum Flux Reactor
Compact fuelled FE generator. Right-click with Dilithium for a standard charge or Trilithium for a hotter high-output charge. The reactor exposes Forge Energy and actively pushes into adjacent receivers, including the existing Heavy Energy Cable network. It stores up to 4,000,000 FE.

Heat is an intentional operational constraint. Above 70% heat an unprotected player within eight blocks receives weakness and disorientation. Critical heat also causes periodic damage.

## Environmental Regulator
Powered laboratory safety system. It accepts FE, consumes 40 FE/t while active, and establishes a twelve-block protection radius. Protected players are immune to the Quantum Flux Reactor exposure effects implemented by the overhaul hazard layer.

## Wall Terminal
Interactive local diagnostic decoration. Right-click to count nearby FE-capable block entities and report how many currently contain energy.

## Decorative family
Tritanium Bulkhead Panel, Holographic Floor Panel, Blue Hazard Panel, Reactor Casing and Industrial Ceiling Grid provide a consistent laboratory/ship interior palette without depending on generated structures.
