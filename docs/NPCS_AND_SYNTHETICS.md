# Village specialists and synthetic threats

Matter Overdrive progression no longer relies on generated MO structures.

- Field Scientists can naturally appear around vanilla villages. They provide progression-aware dialogue, PDA access and Dilithium sample exchange.
- Field Scientists can issue the existing `matter_scanner` and `matter_decomposer` research contracts. The first contact and each assignment are recorded in the player's PDA field log.
- Systems Engineers can naturally appear around villages. They explain the real power/matter/network separation, trade practical circuits/batteries, and issue the existing `network_backbone` and `heavy_distribution` contracts.
- The existing Mad Scientist interaction chain is unchanged and is also allowed to appear naturally around villages at a lower rate.
- The Mad Scientist remains the long-form conversion/research contact. It shares the same PDA discovery log, so specialist conversations form one player-facing lore trail instead of a second quest system.
- Assimilators are durable collective-style synthetics whose melee attacks weaken targets.
- Phase Stalkers briefly phase out and accelerate while pursuing a target.

Village specialists are created through a bounded village population hook, not by bringing the retired Matter Overdrive structures back into world generation.
