# Reactor Progress

## Current work

- Added a guide-only world overlay for the fusion reactor.
- Green boxes indicate valid blocks.
- Yellow boxes indicate empty required positions.
- Red boxes indicate incorrect blocks.
- Cyan boxes show the anomaly centre and its accepted vertical range.
- Reduced guide image sizing so the four pages have room for their text.

## Usage

Hold the Fusion Reactor Assembly Guide and look directly at the reactor controller. The expected placement boxes render around the controller. The overlay is client-side and does not alter structure validation.
