# Power and Machines

Matter Overdrive machines use Forge Energy. Solar Panels provide straightforward early generation. Heavy Energy Cables distribute FE between compatible blocks, and the Charging Station recharges compatible portable energy items.

Later systems have larger energy demands. Upgrade machines deliberately rather than assuming that every speed upgrade is free: faster operation generally increases the rate at which a machine can consume its stored resources.
