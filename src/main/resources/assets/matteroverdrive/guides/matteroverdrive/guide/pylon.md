# Dimensional Pylon

The authentic Dimensional Pylon is a **2 x 3 x 2 structure of 12 Pylon blocks**. Once formed, the assembly becomes a shared machine with dimensional-rift generation, charge state, matter storage and Forge Energy storage/output.

The restored backend holds up to **2,048 matter** and **1,000,000 FE**, with up to **2,048 FE/t** output. Matter is consumed by generation according to the dimensional field.

For save compatibility, an unformed single Pylon continues to provide the earlier channel-based wireless item-network relay. Forming the full structure switches the assembly to Dimensional Pylon behavior.
