# Specialists and Lore

Matter Overdrive's research story is delivered through people in the world. The
specialists can appear near vanilla villages, so the lore is discovered during
survival play rather than hidden in a separate menu.

## Field Scientist

Field Scientists catalogue reproducible experiments: scanning blocks, measuring
Matter and building the first decomposer. Speak with one to receive the existing
Matter Technology assignments. A first conversation records the contact in the
PDA field log; repeated conversations do not create duplicate lore entries.

## Systems Engineer

Systems Engineers maintain the practical boundary between FE cables, Matter
Transport Pipe and Network Cable. They issue the existing network and heavy-power
assignments, and their dialogue explains why a machine can have energy while a
replication route is still disconnected.

## Mad Scientist

The Mad Scientist remains the conversion and long-form story host. Puny Humans,
the legacy scientist programme, the modern research campaign and the optional
Cocktail of Ascension retain their existing persistent contract state. Speaking
with the scientist also records the contact and advances any matching conversation
objective.

## The PDA field log

Use a Data Pad in the air to review:

- current research stage and the next assignment;
- first-contact records and unique specialist lore;
- block scans and measured Matter values;
- active contracts, progress, rewards and safe abandonment.

The PDA is the gameplay record. GuideME remains the technical manual: use the
manual for block recipes and system instructions, and use the PDA to remember
what your character has learned and what must be done next.

## Discovery loop

Village specialist -> PDA contact record -> existing contract objective -> field
experiment -> Contract Market redemption -> next specialist brief.

The specialists use the existing custom 64x64 Minecraft skin textures packaged in
the mod for consistent silhouettes and readable roles. They are rendered as
distinct village characters rather than reskinned vanilla villagers.
