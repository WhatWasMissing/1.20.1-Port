# Matter Technology

The matter chain is **analyze -> store pattern -> supply matter and FE -> replicate**.

The Decomposer converts supported items into matter. The Matter Analyzer and Scanner discover item information used by the pattern system. Pattern Drives and Pattern Storage preserve patterns. The Replicator consumes stored matter and Forge Energy to manufacture the selected pattern. Matter Pipes and containers move/store matter where their exposed interfaces allow it.
