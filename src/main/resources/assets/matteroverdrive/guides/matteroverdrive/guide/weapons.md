# Weapons

Matter Overdrive energy weapons consume their own stored energy or supported portable Matter Overdrive energy sources. Unrelated energy weapons are not batteries.

Use the Weapon Station to configure supported weapon modules, batteries and presentation options. Weapon heat, reload behavior and energy consumption remain gameplay constraints in Survival even when the same weapon is easier to configure in Creative.
