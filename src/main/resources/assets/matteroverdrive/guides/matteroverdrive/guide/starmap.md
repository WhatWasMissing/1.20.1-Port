# Star Map

The Star Map provides the strategic layer: Galaxy -> Quadrant -> Star -> Planet. Journeys persist, use different same-star/interstellar travel scales, and can trigger encounters and fleet combat.

Scout and colonizer ships support exploration and colonization. Colonies have economy and build-queue state; strategic choices persist with the world rather than being a disposable GUI simulation.
