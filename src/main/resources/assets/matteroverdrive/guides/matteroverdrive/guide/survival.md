# Survival Progression

Matter Overdrive has a suggested research path, but **quests are not a mandatory technology gate**. The Mad Scientist campaign exists to teach systems, provide story and give useful rewards. Experienced players can follow recipes and material requirements directly.

Suggested path:

**Scientist Research -> Matter Technology -> Automation & Drones -> Advanced Power -> Fusion Research -> Anomaly Engineering**

See [World Structures](structures.md) for the exploration sites that support this path.

## Mining

Tritanium and Dilithium ores generate in the Overworld. Both are pickaxe-mined resources and require an iron-tier tool or better for their normal drops. Fortune and Silk Touch are handled by their block loot tables.

Finding a Mad Scientist first is helpful but not required. If you obtain the materials yourself, you may begin building Matter Overdrive technology immediately.

## Optional scientist route

A **Mad Scientist House** is the intended discoverable entry point for a first playthrough. Scientists offer a guided research campaign beginning with recovered legacy assignments and then point toward the major technology families.

Completing those assignments should reward exploration and teach the machines. It should not be necessary to unlock the recipes themselves.

## First power

Smelt your first ore resources and work toward a Solar Panel. Connect machines with Heavy Energy Cable where required. The Charging Station is the normal early way to recharge Matter Overdrive batteries and other compatible Forge Energy items.

## First matter

The Decomposer converts suitable items into stored matter. Matter is then used by the replication system together with analyzed patterns. Pattern Drives and Pattern Storage provide persistent pattern handling; the Replicator consumes energy and matter to manufacture selected patterns.

This is the core **Matter Technology** loop. A veteran player may build it immediately; a new player can use scientist assignments and GuideME as a checklist.

## Automation

Matter Overdrive machines expose standard Forge capabilities where appropriate. This means normal Forge automation can interact with public machine inventories, energy stores and matter interfaces without requiring a specific logistics mod.

Matter Overdrive's own pipes, routers, switches, pattern systems and drone technology form the suggested next stage once manual matter processing is established.

## Advanced power and fusion

As your machine network grows, move from basic generation and charging toward energy storage/distribution and eventually the Fusion Reactor. Fusion is expensive because of its material and infrastructure requirements, not because a scientist has granted permission to craft it.

## Anomaly engineering

Natural Gravitational Anomalies can be discovered while exploring. They are dangerous and are most useful after learning stabilizer and fusion technology. Finding one early is therefore a reason to mark its coordinates, not a requirement to rush the endgame.

## Exploration

Matter Overdrive retains optional alternate discoveries for existing generated sites, but authored structures are dormant in new worlds in 0.7:

- Scientist Houses introduce the guided campaign.
- Crashed Ships and Sand Pits provide early exploration/salvage targets.
- Android Houses are combat-oriented sites.
- Cargo Ships and Underwater Bases are rarer large facilities.
- Natural Gravitational Anomalies foreshadow the late game and remain naturally available.

New players do not need to locate an MO-authored structure. Technology acquisition, vanilla structure loot, village contacts, combat/mining events, Field Operations and recovered lore fragments continue the technology tree.
